# VMV Copy Source Audit V05

Authority: original `www.vmvnetwork.com` single-page site supplied by the owner.

## Finding

V04 retained the company identity, service categories, team roster, locations, and phone number, but compressed and replaced too much of the original public copy with newly written positioning language.

## V05 correction

- Restored the original public tagline: `Building new communities to empower endless possibilities!`
- Restored the original About Us narrative with only punctuation/capitalization cleanup.
- Restored the three original service descriptions rather than substituting short capability summaries.
- Restored original navigation labels: About Us / Services / Team / Contact Us.
- Restored the original service-availability wording.
- Restored the complete team roster and roles.
- Restored the original addresses, phone number, extension, and ICT company descriptor.

## Copy law going forward

Original owner-supplied copy is source authority. Improve grammar, hierarchy, and readability, but do not replace substantive claims, positioning, service descriptions, or terminology with newly invented studio copy unless explicitly approved.
